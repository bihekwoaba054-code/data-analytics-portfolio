SQL case study

Economic Impact & Inventory Analysis

A retail analysis designed around business questions rather than isolated queries: inflation sensitivity, GDP conditions, promotional effectiveness and inventory risk.

Questions investigated

  • Which product categories suffer the largest sales-volume decline when inflation rises?
  • How much cash is tied up in unsold inventory during low-GDP months?
  • Do promotional campaigns produce a meaningful sales lift?
  • Which products combine heavy inventory with little sales activity?
  • Which months repeatedly carry the highest unsold stock?
SELECT
    s.sales_month,
    SUM(s.inventoryquantity) AS unsold_inventory,
    SUM(s.inventoryquantity * s.productcost)
        AS inventory_value
FROM Sales s
GROUP BY s.sales_month
ORDER BY unsold_inventory DESC;
